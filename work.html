the work

Houses, halls and storefronts around Greater Burlington. Most of these photos were taken by Gabe on the last day of the job.

Sigma Phi House

Burlington, VT · Columns, trim & entry

Four Corinthian columns, a full pediment and a fanlight entry, restored to read crisp against red brick.

02

Queen Anne Victorian

Greater Burlington · Exterior repaint

Spindles, brackets and fish-scale shingle, each one scraped, primed and cut in by hand, lead-safe from the first scrape.

03

Lake Cottage

Lake Champlain · Full exterior & porch

A lakeside camp in deep lake blue, with white rails and a screened porch ready for another fifty summers.

04

Red Shingle Colonial

Chittenden County · Shingle & trim

Cedar shingle in barn red, black shutters and white trim, back-brushed course by course.

05

Library & Community Hall

Greater Burlington · Full interior

Bright, scrubbable walls against exposed timber, sequenced room by room so the building stayed open.

06

Auto Dealership

Chittenden County · Facade & canopy

Metal panel, canopy and soffit, prepped and coated off-hours so the lot never closed.

07

Built-ins & Porch

Greater Burlington · Cabinetry & porch floor

A slate built-in with matching wainscot, and a porch floor laid out in a two-grey diamond.
