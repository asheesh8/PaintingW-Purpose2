our story

good workshould dogood.

Painting With Purpose is a Greater Burlington painting company with one big goal: helping end homelessness in Vermont.

how it started

Painting is honest work. You can see it from the street. We wanted the why behind it to be just as visible.

Painting With Purpose started with a crew, a van and a promise: do excellent work for our neighbors, and use the business to help the neighbors who don’t have a home at all.

Two years and more than a hundred jobs later, that promise still sets how we work. We show up on time with everything we need. We protect your home, clean up daily and talk you through each step. And a share of every job goes straight to CVOEO.

Along the way we were honored with the Joy and Jerry Meyers Cup, which meant a lot to a young company still finding its feet.

our purpose

helping endhomelessnessin vermont.

We partner with the Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity (CVOEO), which helps Vermonters find and keep safe, stable housing. When you hire us, part of what you pay goes to that work. No extra cost to you.

meet gabe& zach.

Brothers Gabe and Zach Dunn started Painting With Purpose to prove a painting company could be really good at the work and really good for the community.

Between the two of them, one is on nearly every job: walking the estimate, checking the prep, or out at a town event talking paint colors and CVOEO with anyone who stops by the tent. Gabe also shoots a lot of the project photos you see on this site.
