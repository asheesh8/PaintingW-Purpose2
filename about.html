Our story

Started by two brothers in Burlington.

Gabe and Zach Dunn wanted a painting company that did honest, careful work and gave something back to the place they live.

A share of every job goes to CVOEO to help Vermonters find housing. More than $15,000 so far, and over 100 jobs.
