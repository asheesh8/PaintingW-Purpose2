A Greater Burlington painting company with one larger goal: helping end homelessness in Vermont.

Queen Anne Victorian · Burlington

(story)

Painting is honest work. You can see it from the street. We wanted the reason behind it to be just as easy to see.

Painting With Purpose opened in February 2022 with a simple idea: do excellent work for our neighbors, and use the business to help the neighbors who have no home at all.

The plan took first place at the Joy and Jerry Meyers Cup at UVM. More than a hundred jobs later, it still sets how we work. On time, fully equipped, your home protected, and the site cleaned every day.

(cvoeo)

part of every job goes to housing.

CVOEO, the Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity, helps people in shelters move into permanent homes. Our share covers the costs that stand in the way: a security deposit, a first month’s rent, the utilities.
