(purpose)

good work should do good

A Greater Burlington painting company with one big goal: helping end homelessness in Vermont.

(story)

Painting is honest work. You can see it from the street. We wanted the why behind it to be just as visible.

Painting With Purpose started with a crew, a van and a promise: do excellent work for our neighbors, and use the business to help the neighbors who don’t have a home at all.

More than a hundred jobs later, that promise still sets how we work. We show up on time with everything we need, protect your home, clean up daily and talk you through each step. Along the way we were honored with the Joy and Jerry Meyers Cup.

(cvoeo)

part of every job goes to housing.

The Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity helps Vermonters find and keep safe, stable housing. When you hire us, a share of what you pay goes to that work, at no extra cost to you.

  1. You book a job
  2. We do it right
  3. A share goes to CVOEO
  4. A neighbor finds a home

(how we work)

what you can count on

  1. 01

    Quality you can see

    Every painter inspects their own work before anyone else does. If it isn’t right, we make it right.

  2. 02

    Trust & respect

    We treat your home like it’s ours. Things covered, nothing left behind.

  3. 03

    Clean, every day

    Brushes washed, drop cloths folded and walkways clear before we leave each afternoon.

  4. 04

    Straight talk

    Step-by-step communication, so you always know what’s happening next and when.

(founders)

gabe & zach

Brothers Gabe and Zach Dunn started Painting With Purpose to prove a painting company could be really good at the work and really good for the community. One of them is on nearly every job, walking the estimate or checking the prep. Gabe also shoots most of the project photos on this site.
