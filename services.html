(questions)

good to know

We cover Addison, Chittenden, Franklin, Grand Isle, Lamoille, Orange and Washington counties. Not sure if you’re in range? Call 802-PAINTER.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We walk the project with you, answer questions and send a written estimate. No cost, no obligation.

How far ahead should I book?

Exterior work books up early in Vermont, so reach out in the spring for summer jobs. Interiors are easier to fit in.

Do you help with color?

Yes. Color consulting is part of the job. We can suggest combinations that suit the house and the light, and paint test swatches before you decide.

What about lead paint in an older home?

We are EPA lead certified and work lead-safe on older homes from the first scrape. We also offer EMP lead inspections.

How does the giving work?

A portion of the profit from every job goes to CVOEO, which helps people in shelters move into permanent housing. It adds nothing to your price.

Do I need to move my furniture?

We move and cover what we can and protect the rest. Put away anything fragile or personal before we arrive.
