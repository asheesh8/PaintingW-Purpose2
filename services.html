How a job runs

  1. Walkthrough

    We visit, listen and look closely at the surfaces.

  2. Written estimate

    A clear price and scope, usually within a few days.

  3. Prep and paint

    Scrape, repair, prime, then two finish coats.

  4. Final walk

    We walk it with you and fix anything you notice.

We work across Greater Burlington: Burlington, South Burlington, Winooski, Essex, Colchester, Williston, Shelburne and nearby towns.

Questions

How much does a house painting job cost?

Every house is different, so we walk it first and send a written estimate. It is free and there is no obligation.

When is the exterior season in Vermont?

Usually May through October, when it is warm and dry enough for paint to cure. Interiors run all year.

Do you handle lead paint on older homes?

Yes. We follow EPA lead-safe practices and can inspect before any work starts.

Where does the giving come from?

A share of every job goes to CVOEO, which helps Vermonters find and keep housing.
