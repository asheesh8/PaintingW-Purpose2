good to know

questions,answered.

Something else on your mind? Call 802-PAINTER and a real person will pick up.

is the estimate really free?

Yes. We walk the project with you, answer questions and send a written estimate. There’s no charge and no obligation.

how far ahead do you book?

Exterior season fills up fast in Vermont, so spring and early summer are best for booking outdoor work. Interiors and commercial jobs run year-round.

do you help pick colors?

Happily. We can bring samples, suggest combinations that suit the house and the light, and paint test swatches before you commit.

what about lead paint in an older home?

Many Vermont homes built before 1978 have lead paint. We follow certified lead-safe (EMP) practices, contain the work area and clean with HEPA equipment.

how does the giving work?

A share of every job goes to the Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity (CVOEO), which helps Vermonters find and keep safe housing.

do I need to move my furniture?

We’ll move and cover what we can and protect everything else. Just put away anything fragile or personal before we arrive.
