out in the community
who we are

A local painting company built on a simple idea: good work should do good.

We paint homes and businesses across Greater Burlington with the kind of care you’d give your own place. Crews arrive when we say, protect everything, and walk the job with you before we call it done.

Then we take a share of every job and send it to the Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity, helping Vermonters find safe, stable housing.
